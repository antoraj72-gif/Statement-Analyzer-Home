Questions people ask

What is Statement Analyzer?

It shows you where your money went. Upload your bank or credit card statement and it sorts every transaction into categories, with charts, money in and money out, your biggest expenses, recurring payments and ten quick insights. It works fully offline on your own device.

Which file should I download from my bank?

The transaction export in CSV or Excel format works best – most net banking sites have a Download or Export option on the account statement page. A text-based PDF statement also works; check the ledger afterwards. If you are not sure of the format, tap Download CSV template in the app.

My PDF statement has a password. Will it work?

Yes. The app asks for the password right there. It is used only once on your device to open the file and is never saved.

Is my bank data safe?

Yes. Your statement is read only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, cloud or AI, and the app is blocked from connecting to any website. Your statements are not even stored in the app.

Some transactions are in the wrong category. How do I fix them?

Go to the Ledger, pick the right category for that row and tap Refresh matching categories. Every transaction with the same description is fixed at once, and the app remembers it for your next statement. If no category fits, choose Add new category.

How do I set a budget?

Open Budget vs Actual, choose a category, type your monthly budget and tap Save budget. The app shows how much you actually spent against each budget.

Can I analyse my credit card statement too?

Yes. Upload the credit card statement separately – it works the same way as a bank statement.

Can I move my categories and budgets to another phone?

Yes. In Export & Settings, tap Export settings to save a small file, then Import settings on the other device. Nothing is uploaded on the way.

Does it need internet?

Only to install it the first time and to get updates. Everything else works with no internet.

What happens when my access days are over?

The app asks for a new unlock key. Send your Device ID again and you receive a renewed key. Your budgets and category corrections stay on your device.
